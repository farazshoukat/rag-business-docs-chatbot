Flame On! Restaurant — FAQ

Q: What are your delivery hours?
A: We deliver daily from 12 PM to 11 PM.

Q: Do you offer vegetarian options?
A: Yes, we have veggie pizzas, pasta, and loaded fries.

Q: What areas do you deliver to?
A: We currently deliver within a 5km radius of our branch.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive a WhatsApp update with status.

Q: Do you accept cash on delivery?
A: Yes, we accept both cash on delivery and online payment.
